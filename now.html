Building

    Campus deployment with FUTA

    An MoU is signed with Federal University Akure as the first institutional deployment partner. The work in front of me is the card-reader hardware, the transaction reconciliation backend, and a wallet a student will actually top up twice.

  • Weyz

    Offline-first payment path

    A bus cannot depend on connectivity at the moment of payment. Most of the interesting engineering is in what happens between the tap and the settlement, when the network is not there.
